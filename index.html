About Us

NYC Resto Finder is a student project that lists restaurants across the five boroughs of New York: Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx, Queens and Staten Island. You can search by name or category, filter by borough, see the best quality ones, and even add, edit, or delete restaurants.
